Inventory Core

Inventory you can trust — always accurate

Ledger-First Architecture

Every stock change is recorded as an immutable movement entry. Current stock is calculated from the ledger — no silent overrides, no data loss, full audit forever.

Lot & Expiry Tracking

Track goods by batch and expiry date. FIFO cost layers ensure accurate COGS — critical for food, pharma, and regulated industries.

Container & Bin Traceability

Every item located at bin level. Trace any SKU to its exact physical position across all warehouses in real time.

WMS — Warehouse Management

Your warehouse, running on autopilot

  • Multi-Warehouse Support — manage unlimited warehouses per tenant, each with its own zone and bin structure
  • Digital Picklists — auto-generated from sales shipments, assigned to staff, tracked through PENDING → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED
  • Putaway Tasks — inbound receipts instantly trigger directed putaway instructions for warehouse staff
  • Cycle Count Plans — schedule physical counts by zone or SKU, system auto-reconciles on submit
  • Inter-Warehouse Transfers — move stock with full approval flow and dual movement entries in the ledger

WMS tasks are created automatically via event triggers — no manual intervention. When a shipment is allocated, a picklist appears in the warehouse app instantly.

Replenishment & Demand Intelligence

Demand Snapshots

Periodic snapshots capture sales velocity per SKU over a defined period. The platform calculates average daily usage and variance to drive data-backed reorder points.

Low-Stock Alerts

Real-time alerts fire when stock falls below the calculated reorder point — surfaced on the dashboard before a stockout happens, with enough lead time to act.

Lot Expiry Alerts

Proactively flag lots approaching expiry — critical for food, pharma, and perishables. Prevents costly write-offs from goods that expire on the shelf undetected.

Trust & Compliance

Enterprise-grade security, built in from day one

Strict Tenant Isolation

Every query is scoped to tenant_id at the repository layer — architecturally impossible to read another tenant's data.

JWT + Refresh Tokens

Short-lived access tokens with long-lived refresh tokens stored in the database. Sessions can be revoked remotely and rotation is enforced on every refresh.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular RBAC with permissions checked on every protected route at the middleware layer — not just at the controller. Roles are tenant-scoped.

User Invite Flow

Invite staff by email with signed tokens. No password sharing, no manual account creation — users self-onboard securely with enforced email verification.

Full Audit Trail

Every meaningful action is logged to user_activities with timestamp, user, and tenant context. Compliance-ready out of the box.

Parameterized Queries Only

Zero raw SQL string interpolation across the entire codebase. SQL injection is structurally prevented — no guard clauses needed because the attack surface does not exist.

Architecture Principles
  • Clean layered architecture: Route → Controller → Service → Repository → DB
  • Controllers are thin — only HTTP concerns and input validation
  • Services own all business logic and transaction boundaries
  • Repositories are pure persistence — no business rules
  • Multi-step writes always wrapped in Unit of Work — atomic or fully rolled back
  • EventBus decouples WMS task creation from sales and purchase flows
  • Composite indexes on all tenant-scoped queries for isolated query plans
  • Row-level locking prevents concurrent stock corruption under load
